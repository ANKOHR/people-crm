# Lessons for KOML

An explicit follow-up date is enough to compute a useful queue, but it cannot establish relationship quality or whether someone needs attention. Missing interaction data means missing records, not absence of a relationship. Scheduling counts partition contacts; missing-history counts overlap them and must not be added to the total.

The synthetic tests validate the assumption that deterministic date rules can recover planted cases with exact counts and no false positives. A corrupted planted date changes the report, confirming computations are driven by input. The assumption that Henry will use the tool daily remains unvalidated until the two-week trial.

Operating rule for future builds: require explicit observation dates and independent case-level ground truth before making aggregate accuracy claims. Keep missing data separate from inferred facts, and keep real usefulness claims pending until real use has been measured.
